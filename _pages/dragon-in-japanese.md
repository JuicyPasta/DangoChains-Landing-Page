<h1>Dragon in Japanese: 竜 / 龍</h1>
<p>"Dragon" is <span lang="ja">竜</span>, read <span lang="ja">りゅう</span> (<i>ryū</i>) or <span lang="ja">たつ</span> (<i>tatsu</i>). <span lang="ja">龍</span> is the older, more elaborate form of the same kanji, popular in names, temples and tattoos. Western-style dragons (wings, fire) are usually <span lang="ja">ドラゴン</span> (<i>doragon</i>); the Japanese dragon is a long, wingless water god that brings rain.</p>
