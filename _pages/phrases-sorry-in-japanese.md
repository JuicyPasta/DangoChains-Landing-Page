<h1>How to say sorry in Japanese</h1>
<p>Japanese has three everyday apologies. <span lang="ja">ごめんなさい</span> (<i>gomen nasai</i>) is a real "I'm sorry", said to friends and family (shortened to <span lang="ja">ごめん</span>). <span lang="ja">すみません</span> (<i>sumimasen</i>) is the polite all-purpose one: sorry, excuse me, and even thank you. <span lang="ja">申し訳ありません</span> (<i>mōshiwake arimasen</i>) is the formal apology you hear from businesses and at work.</p>

<tr><td lang="ja">ごめんなさい</td><td>gomen nasai</td><td>neutral</td><td>a sincere apology to people close to you</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">ごめん</td><td>gomen</td><td>casual</td><td>sorry, to friends</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">ごめんね</td><td>gomen ne</td><td>casual, soft</td><td>a gentle sorry</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">すみません</td><td>sumimasen</td><td>polite</td><td>sorry or excuse me to strangers, staff and teachers</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">すいません</td><td>suimasen</td><td>polite, spoken</td><td>a relaxed way of saying the same</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">失礼しました</td><td>shitsurei shimashita</td><td>polite</td><td>"pardon me" for a small slip</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">申し訳ありません</td><td>mōshiwake arimasen</td><td>formal</td><td>apologising to a boss or customer</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">申し訳ございません</td><td>mōshiwake gozaimasen</td><td>very formal</td><td>customer service, announcements</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">悪い</td><td>warui</td><td>casual</td><td>"my bad"; mostly men</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">お詫び申し上げます</td><td>o-wabi mōshiagemasu</td><td>very formal, written</td><td>official apologies and letters</td></tr>
</tbody></table>
