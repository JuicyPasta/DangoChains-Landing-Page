<h1>Dog in Japanese: 犬 (inu)</h1>
<p>"Dog" is <span lang="ja">犬</span>, read <span lang="ja">いぬ</span> (<i>inu</i>). Children call dogs <span lang="ja">わんちゃん</span>, and dogs say <span lang="ja">ワンワン</span> (<i>wan wan</i>). In compounds 犬 is read <i>ken</i>: <span lang="ja">番犬</span> (<i>banken</i>, guard dog), <span lang="ja">愛犬</span> (<i>aiken</i>, a beloved pet dog).</p>
