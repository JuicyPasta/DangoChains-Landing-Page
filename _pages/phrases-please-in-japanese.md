<h1>How to say please in Japanese</h1>
<p>Japanese has two "please"s for asking and one for offering. <span lang="ja">ください</span> (<i>kudasai</i>) and <span lang="ja">お願いします</span> (<i>onegai shimasu</i>) ask for something; <span lang="ja">どうぞ</span> (<i>dōzo</i>) offers: "please, go ahead", "here you are".</p>
<table class="st-table"><thead><tr><th>Japanese</th><th>Romaji</th><th>Use</th><th>Example</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td lang="ja">ください</td><td>kudasai</td><td>asking for a thing (after を)</td><td><span lang="ja">水をください</span>: water, please</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">〜てください</td><td>-te kudasai</td><td>asking someone to do something</td><td><span lang="ja">ゆっくり話してください</span>: please speak slowly</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">お願いします</td><td>onegai shimasu</td><td>requests and orders; a little softer and more polite</td><td><span lang="ja">コーヒーをお願いします</span>: a coffee, please</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">どうぞ</td><td>dōzo</td><td>offering: go ahead, help yourself, here you are</td><td><span lang="ja">どうぞ、座ってください</span>: please, have a seat</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">〜ないでください</td><td>-naide kudasai</td><td>please don't</td><td><span lang="ja">写真を撮らないでください</span>: please don't take photos</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">お願い</td><td>onegai</td><td>casual: please!</td><td><span lang="ja">お願い、手伝って</span>: please help me</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">〜てくださいませんか</td><td>-te kudasaimasen ka</td><td>very polite request</td><td><span lang="ja">教えてくださいませんか</span>: would you tell me?</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">どうか</td><td>dōka</td><td>earnest "please" (formal or emotional)</td><td><span lang="ja">どうかお願いします</span>: I beg you</td></tr>
</tbody></table>
