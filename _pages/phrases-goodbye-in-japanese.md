<h1>How to say goodbye in Japanese</h1>
<p><span lang="ja">さようなら</span> (<i>sayōnara</i>) is the goodbye in every textbook, but adults use it less than you'd think: it can sound final, as if you won't meet again soon. Children say it to their teachers at the end of the school day. Between friends, goodbye is usually <span lang="ja">じゃあね</span> or <span lang="ja">またね</span> ("see you"), and at work it is <span lang="ja">失礼します</span> or <span lang="ja">お先に失礼します</span>.</p>
