# What's New

<section class="release" markdown="1">
<span class="tag">Latest</span>

## Version 1.0.4

App Store: November 10, 2026. Google Play: November 10, 2026 (to everyone from November 11).

- Every word explained: after each game, Word Groups shows each word's reading and English meaning
- Every puzzle rewritten, and new puzzles and fixes now arrive over the air, without an app update; the built-in puzzles still work offline
- Shares carry the puzzle number and a link that opens the same board in a browser, so friends can play it
- Normal and Hard start with furigana off, since their groups often turn on readings (one switch in Settings turns it on)
- After your first win, the app offers a daily reminder once

</section>

<section class="release" markdown="1">

## Version 1.0.3

App Store: November 9, 2026.

- Redesigned Collection: every dango has a name, its own look, and a "How to unlock" hint, even while locked
- Fairer achievements: streak and milestone dangos count per difficulty, Clutch is a win on your last try, Comeback covers 3–4 mistakes
- Dango names in Japanese, and tidier layouts on small screens and large text sizes
- After a few wins, the app asks once whether you'd like to rate it

</section>

<section class="release" markdown="1">

## Version 1.0.1

Google Play: November 7, 2026. App Store: November 8, 2026.

- Share your result as an emoji dango grid, with no spoilers
- After every game, review all four word groups with kana readings
- Hard difficulty is now available every day
- A quick how-to-play guide on first launch
- Optional daily reminder (off by default; turn it on in Settings)
- Archive: jump to a random unplayed puzzle and see each month's progress
- "Done for today" countdown to the next puzzle
- Restore Purchases and Privacy Policy in Settings
- Japanese text, hints and stats are fully localized
- Bug fixes and polish

</section>

## Version 1.0.0

January 2026

- A new Japanese word-grouping puzzle every day: sort 16 words into four groups of four
- Furigana above every kanji, with an option to turn it off
- Streaks, stats and a collection of dango to earn
- An archive of past puzzles, unlocked with a one-time purchase
