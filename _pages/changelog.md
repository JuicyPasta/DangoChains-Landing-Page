# What's New

### `Latest`
## Version 1.0.1
Google Play: November 7, 2026. App Store: coming soon.

- Share your result as an emoji dango grid, with no spoilers
- After every game, review all four word groups with kana readings
- Hard difficulty is now available every day
- A quick how-to-play guide on first launch
- Optional daily reminder (off by default; turn it on in Settings)
- Archive: jump to a random unplayed puzzle and see each month's progress
- "Done for today" countdown to the next puzzle
- Restore Purchases and Privacy Policy in Settings
- Japanese text, hints and stats are fully localized
- Bug fixes and polish
