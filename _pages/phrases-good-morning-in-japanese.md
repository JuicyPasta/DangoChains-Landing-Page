<h1>How to say good morning in Japanese</h1>
<p>Good morning is <span lang="ja">おはようございます</span> (<i>ohayō gozaimasu</i>), or <span lang="ja">おはよう</span> (<i>ohayō</i>) to friends and family. The answer is the same phrase back. It comes from <span lang="ja">お早う</span>, "it's early", and is written in hiragana. Use it until about 10 or 11 a.m.; after that, switch to <span lang="ja">こんにちは</span>. In TV, restaurants and some other workplaces, <span lang="ja">おはようございます</span> is the first greeting of the shift whatever the time.</p>

<tr><td lang="ja">おはようございます</td><td>ohayō gozaimasu</td><td>polite</td><td>teachers, colleagues, neighbours, anyone older</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">おはよう</td><td>ohayō</td><td>casual</td><td>friends, family, classmates</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">おっはー</td><td>ohhā</td><td>slang</td><td>jokey; a 2000s TV catchphrase</td></tr>
</tbody></table>
