<p>64 of the most useful Japanese words to start with, grouped by theme, each with its kanji, the reading in kana, romaji and an English meaning. Almost all are JLPT N5, the first level. Learn the kana reading rather than only the romaji: it is what you will read on signs, menus and in apps. Then see the words in use in a daily puzzle.</p>
