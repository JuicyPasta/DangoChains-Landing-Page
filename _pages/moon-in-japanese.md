<h1>Moon in Japanese: 月 (tsuki)</h1>
<p>"Moon" is <span lang="ja">月</span>, read <span lang="ja">つき</span> (<i>tsuki</i>). The same kanji means "month" (read <i>gatsu</i> or <i>getsu</i>: <span lang="ja">一月</span> <i>ichigatsu</i>, January) and starts Monday, <span lang="ja">月曜日</span> (<i>getsuyōbi</i>, "moon day"). In Japan people see a rabbit pounding mochi on the moon, not a face.</p>
