<h1>How to say happy birthday in Japanese</h1>
<p>Happy birthday is <span lang="ja">お誕生日おめでとう</span> (<i>o-tanjōbi omedetō</i>) to friends and family, and <span lang="ja">お誕生日おめでとうございます</span> (<i>o-tanjōbi omedetō gozaimasu</i>) to a teacher, boss or anyone you speak politely to. <span lang="ja">おめでとう</span> alone means "congratulations". At parties people usually sing the song in English: <span lang="ja">ハッピーバースデー</span>.</p>
<table class="st-table"><thead><tr><th>Japanese</th><th>Romaji</th><th>Politeness</th><th>When to use it</th></tr></thead><tbody>
<tr><td lang="ja">お誕生日おめでとうございます</td><td>o-tanjōbi omedetō gozaimasu</td><td>polite</td><td>teachers, colleagues, customers</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">誕生日おめでとう</td><td>tanjōbi omedetō</td><td>casual</td><td>friends, family, partners</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">ハッピーバースデー</td><td>happī bāsudē</td><td>casual</td><td>from English; cards and the song</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">おめでとう</td><td>omedetō</td><td>casual</td><td>congratulations</td></tr>
</tbody></table>
