<h1>How to say welcome in Japanese</h1>
<p>Which "welcome" depends on where you are. Shop and restaurant staff call out <span lang="ja">いらっしゃいませ</span>; you don't need to answer. To welcome someone to a place, say <span lang="ja">ようこそ</span> (<span lang="ja">日本へようこそ</span>, "welcome to Japan"). And to family coming home, <span lang="ja">おかえり</span>.</p>

<tr><td lang="ja">いらっしゃいませ</td><td>irasshaimase</td><td>very polite</td><td>staff to customers in shops and restaurants</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">いらっしゃい</td><td>irasshai</td><td>casual</td><td>welcoming a guest to your home</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">ようこそ</td><td>yōkoso</td><td>polite</td><td>welcome (to a place or event)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">おかえりなさい</td><td>okaeri nasai</td><td>neutral</td><td>welcome home (reply to ただいま)</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">歓迎します</td><td>kangei shimasu</td><td>formal</td><td>you are welcome here; we welcome you</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">どういたしまして</td><td>dō itashimashite</td><td>polite</td><td>"you're welcome" after thanks: a different welcome</td></tr>
