<h1>Cute Japanese words</h1>
<p>A lot of Japanese cuteness lives in sound words: doubled syllables like <span lang="ja">ふわふわ</span> and <span lang="ja">もちもち</span> describe how something feels, and they sound soft on purpose. Here are 24 cute words with readings, romaji and meanings, from <span lang="ja">かわいい</span> itself to small animals and sweets.</p>

<tr><td lang="ja">可愛らしい</td><td lang="ja">かわいらしい</td><td>kawairashii</td><td>charming, sweet (a little more refined)</td></tr>

<tr><td lang="ja">ゆるキャラ</td><td lang="ja">ゆるキャラ</td><td>yuru-kyara</td><td>the soft, goofy mascots of towns and companies</td></tr>
</tbody></table>
