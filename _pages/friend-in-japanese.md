<h1>Friend in Japanese: 友達 (tomodachi)</h1>
<p>The everyday word for "friend" is <span lang="ja">友達</span> (<span lang="ja">ともだち</span>, <i>tomodachi</i>). Despite the plural-looking <span lang="ja">達</span>, it means one friend or several. In formal speech and writing, people say <span lang="ja">友人</span> (<i>yūjin</i>).</p>
