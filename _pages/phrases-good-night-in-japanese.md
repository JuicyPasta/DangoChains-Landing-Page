<h1>How to say good night in Japanese</h1>
<p>Before going to bed, or when someone else is about to, say <span lang="ja">おやすみなさい</span> (<i>oyasumi nasai</i>), or <span lang="ja">おやすみ</span> (<i>oyasumi</i>) to friends and family. It comes from <span lang="ja">休む</span>, "to rest". Don't mix it up with <span lang="ja">こんばんは</span> (<i>konbanwa</i>): that means "good evening" and is a hello, said when you meet someone after dark.</p>

<tr><td lang="ja">こんばんは</td><td>konbanwa</td><td>neutral</td><td>good evening, as a greeting when you meet</td></tr>

<tr><td lang="ja">また明日</td><td>mata ashita</td><td>casual</td><td>see you tomorrow</td></tr>
<tr><td lang="ja">お先に失礼します</td><td>o-saki ni shitsurei shimasu</td><td>polite</td><td>leaving an evening event or work before others</td></tr>
