<h1>How to say hello in Japanese</h1>
<p><span lang="ja">こんにちは</span> (<i>konnichiwa</i>) is the hello everyone learns first, but it is a daytime greeting, used roughly from late morning until dusk. In the morning people say <span lang="ja">おはようございます</span>, in the evening <span lang="ja">こんばんは</span>, and on the phone <span lang="ja">もしもし</span>. Close friends rarely say <span lang="ja">こんにちは</span> to each other at all; they say <span lang="ja">おはよう</span>, <span lang="ja">やあ</span> or just each other's name. Note the spelling: the last sound of <span lang="ja">こんにちは</span> is written with <span lang="ja">は</span> but read <i>wa</i>.</p>

<p>To start talking to a stranger, for example to ask the way, Japanese uses <span lang="ja">すみません</span> (<i>sumimasen</i>, "excuse me") rather than hello.</p>
