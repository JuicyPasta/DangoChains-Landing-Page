<h1>How to say cheers in Japanese: 乾杯 (kanpai)</h1>
<p>Cheers is <span lang="ja">乾杯</span> (<i>kanpai</i>), literally "dry the cup". At a work party nobody drinks until everyone has a glass and someone leads the <span lang="ja">乾杯</span>, often after <span lang="ja">お疲れさまでした</span> ("thanks for your hard work"). Pour for others rather than yourself, and hold your glass up when someone pours for you.</p>
