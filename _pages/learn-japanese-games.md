# Free games to learn Japanese

Short daily games that teach Japanese vocabulary the way puzzles do: by making you notice how words connect. They all play in your browser with no account, and every game ends with the readings and English meanings of the words you played.

## DangoChains daily

Sixteen Japanese words, four hidden groups of four, six tries. Three boards a day: Easy (everyday words, furigana on), Normal (roughly JLPT N4–N3) and Hard (N2 and up, with idioms). The groups play on how Japanese works: counters (squid are counted with 杯, like a bowl of rice), unusual readings, words that hide other words.

## 熟語 Bridge

Find the one kanji that completes four words: 天□, □分, 元□ and □持ち all take 気. Three boards a day, from JLPT N5 to N2.

The DangoChains app for iPhone and Android has the same daily boards, plus streaks, a dango collection and an optional daily reminder. It's free; the archive of 900+ past puzzles is a one-time unlock, with no subscription.
