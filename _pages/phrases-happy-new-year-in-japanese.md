<h1>Happy New Year in Japanese, and the words of お正月</h1>
<p>From January 1, say <span lang="ja">あけましておめでとうございます</span> (<i>akemashite omedetō gozaimasu</i>), usually followed by <span lang="ja">今年もよろしくお願いします</span> ("I look forward to another year with you"). Before the new year, in late December, the parting words are <span lang="ja">良いお年を</span> (<i>yoi otoshi o</i>, "have a good new year"). New Year (<span lang="ja">お正月</span>) is Japan's biggest family holiday; 2027 is the year of the sheep (<span lang="ja">未年</span>, <i>hitsuji-doshi</i>).</p>

<tr><td lang="ja">あけおめ</td><td>akeome</td><td>slang</td><td>short form in messages to friends</td></tr>

<tr><td lang="ja">良いお年を</td><td>yoi otoshi o</td><td>neutral</td><td>the last goodbye of the year, before January 1</td></tr>

</tbody></table>
