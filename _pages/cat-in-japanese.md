<h1>Cat in Japanese: 猫 (neko)</h1>
<p>"Cat" is <span lang="ja">猫</span>, read <span lang="ja">ねこ</span> (<i>neko</i>). It's often written in hiragana or katakana (<span lang="ja">ネコ</span>) in casual text. Cats say <span lang="ja">にゃー</span> (<i>nyā</i>), and a lot of everyday Japanese is built on them.</p>
