<h1>Merry Christmas in Japanese</h1>
<p>Merry Christmas is simply <span lang="ja">メリークリスマス</span> (<i>merī kurisumasu</i>). Christmas in Japan isn't a religious or family holiday, and December 25 is a normal working day. Christmas Eve is a night for couples, families order a strawberry <span lang="ja">クリスマスケーキ</span>, and fried chicken from KFC has been a Christmas dinner since the 1970s. The family holiday comes a week later: <a href="/phrases/happy-new-year-in-japanese/">New Year</a>.</p>
