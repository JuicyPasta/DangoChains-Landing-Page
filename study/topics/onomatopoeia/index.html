Japanese onomatopoeia (擬音語)

Japanese has a set word for most sounds: a cow says モー, a frog ケロケロ, and a frying pan goes ジュージュー. Each group below is four sound words that belong together. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 9 so far, newest first.

Common Japanese onomatopoeia

Japanese has two kinds: 擬音語 imitate real sounds (a dog's ワンワン, rain's ザーザー), and 擬態語 describe states and feelings that make no sound (キラキラ for sparkle, ドキドキ for a racing heart). Many are doubled, and most work as adverbs: 雨がザーザー降る.
