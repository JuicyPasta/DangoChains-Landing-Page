On'yomi and kun'yomi (onyomi vs kunyomi) of common kanji

Most kanji have two kinds of reading: the on'yomi (音読み), borrowed from Chinese, and the kun'yomi (訓読み), a native Japanese word. As a rule of thumb, a kanji inside a compound of several kanji takes its on'yomi (学生 がくせい), and a kanji on its own or followed by kana takes its kun'yomi (生きる いきる). Dictionaries write on'yomi in katakana and kun'yomi in hiragana, with the kana that follow in parentheses.
