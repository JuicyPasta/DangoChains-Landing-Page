Basic colors in Japanese

Only six colors have an い-adjective form: 赤い, 青い, 白い, 黒い, 黄色い and 茶色い. The others take の before a noun (緑の車, a green car). Many color names are "thing + 色": 水色 is water color, 灰色 ash color.
