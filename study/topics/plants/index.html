Flower names in Japanese

Many flower names have kanji that everyday text skips: バラ, ユリ and たんぽぽ are more common than 薔薇, 百合 and 蒲公英. Japan follows the year by its flowers: 梅 and 桜 in spring, 紫陽花 in the rainy season, 朝顔 and 向日葵 in summer, 菊 and コスモス in autumn.

Trees in Japanese

The word for tree, 木, reads き on its own but もく or ぼく in compounds: 木曜日 (Thursday), 大木 (a big tree). 林 (two trees) is a grove and 森 (three trees) a forest.

Parts of a plant, and what plants do

Flowers 咲く (bloom) and 散る (fall); plants are watered with 水をやる. 花 changes meaning in compounds: 花火 (はなび) is fireworks, not a flower.
