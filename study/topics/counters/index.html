Japanese counters

Japanese counts things with counter words chosen by shape or kind: 本 for long thin things, 枚 for flat ones, 羽 for birds (and, traditionally, rabbits). Each group below is four words that take the same counter. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 70 so far, newest first.

Common Japanese counters

The counter goes after the number, and the two often change sound together: 一本 is いっぽん, 三本 is さんぼん. When you don't know the counter, the native つ series works for most things up to nine.
