Days of the week, months and dates in Japanese

The weekdays are named after the sun, moon and five elements, the months are just numbers, and the days of the month have their own readings up to the tenth. The groups below are calendar words from past puzzles. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 14 so far, newest first.

Days of the week in Japanese

Each weekday is a kanji plus 曜日 (ようび): the sun, the moon and the five elements. In schedules and on calendars the kanji alone is enough: 月 for Monday, 土日 for the weekend.

DayReadingMeaning
月曜日げつようびMonday (moon)
火曜日かようびTuesday (fire)
水曜日すいようびWednesday (water)
木曜日もくようびThursday (wood)
金曜日きんようびFriday (metal, gold)
土曜日どようびSaturday (earth)
日曜日にちようびSunday (sun)

Months in Japanese

Months are the numbers 1 to 12 plus 月 (がつ). Three take the less common number reading: 四月, 七月 and 九月.

MonthReadingMeaning
一月いちがつJanuary
二月にがつFebruary
三月さんがつMarch
四月しがつApril (never よんがつ)
五月ごがつMay
六月ろくがつJune
七月しちがつJuly (not なながつ)
八月はちがつAugust
九月くがつSeptember (not きゅうがつ)
十月じゅうがつOctober
十一月じゅういちがつNovember
十二月じゅうにがつDecember

Days of the month

Dates use 日, but the 1st to the 10th, the 14th, 20th and 24th have their own readings; the rest are regular (十五日 じゅうごにち). The same words count days: 三日 is also "three days".

DateReadingMeaning
一日ついたちthe 1st (いちにち means "one day")
二日ふつかthe 2nd; two days
三日みっかthe 3rd
四日よっかthe 4th
五日いつかthe 5th
六日むいかthe 6th
七日なのかthe 7th
八日ようかthe 8th
九日ここのかthe 9th
十日とおかthe 10th
十四日じゅうよっかthe 14th
二十日はつかthe 20th
二十四日にじゅうよっかthe 24th
