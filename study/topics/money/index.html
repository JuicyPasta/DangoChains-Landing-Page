Money and shopping in Japanese

Prices, paying, saving and borrowing: the money words of everyday Japanese, including the pair learners mix up, 借りる (to borrow) and 貸す (to lend). Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 36 so far, newest first.

Money and shopping words

借りる and 貸す are a pair: 友達にお金を借りる (borrow money from a friend), 友達にお金を貸す (lend money to a friend). Prices are read with 円: 百円 (ひゃくえん), 千円 (せんえん).
