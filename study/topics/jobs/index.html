Jobs and work in Japanese

Job titles, office life, job hunting (就活) and company ranks: the vocabulary of working in Japan. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 32 so far, newest first.

Jobs and work words

Job titles often end in 員 (a member: 会社員, 店員), 士 (a qualified professional: 弁護士, 消防士) or 師 (a skilled practitioner: 看護師). At work you answer to your 上司 and work beside your 同僚.
