Where to eat, shop, stay and play: place words for getting around a Japanese town, including the shops named with 〜屋 (本屋, 花屋, 八百屋). Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 57 so far, newest first.

Places around town

Many shop names end in 屋 (や), "the shop of": 本屋 sells books, 花屋 flowers. Public buildings often end in 館 (a hall: 図書館, 映画館) or 局 (an office: 郵便局).
