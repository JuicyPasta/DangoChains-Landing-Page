Feelings and emotions in Japanese

Japanese talks about feelings mostly with い-adjectives (嬉しい, 悲しい, 怖い) and a few verbs (怒る, 泣く, 笑う). The groups below are feeling words from past puzzles. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 31 so far, newest first.

Feeling words in Japanese

Feeling adjectives like 嬉しい and 悲しい describe your own feelings. For someone else, Japanese adds そう or がる: 悲しそう (looks sad), 怖がる (to be scared). Note 好き and 嫌い take が: 猫が好き, I like cats.
