Words built from opposites

Japanese often joins two opposites into one word: 売買 (buying and selling), 左右 (left and right), 勝負 (a match: win or lose). Each group below is built on opposites. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 27 so far, newest first.

Common words built from two opposites

Put two opposite kanji together and you often get the whole scale between them: 大小 is size, 寒暖 is temperature, 勝負 is the match itself. Most of these use the on'yomi (Chinese) readings.
