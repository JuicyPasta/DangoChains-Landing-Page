One spelling, two readings, two meanings

These words are written the same but change meaning with their reading, like 人気 (にんき, popularity; ひとけ, signs of people). Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 18 so far, newest first.

Common words with two readings and two meanings

The same kanji spelling can stand for two different words. Only context tells you which one is meant: 市場 is いちば at the fish market and しじょう in the business news.
