Kanji that don't mean what they look like

A kanji's everyday meaning can mislead: 猫背 isn't about cats and 目次 isn't about eyes. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 38 so far, newest first.

Everyday words whose kanji mislead

Reading a word kanji by kanji often points the wrong way. Some spellings are old metaphors, and some (怪我, 素敵, 馬鹿) are ateji, kanji picked for their sound rather than their meaning. Learn these as whole words.
