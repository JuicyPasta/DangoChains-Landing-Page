The four seasons in Japanese

Japanese marks the seasons with its own vocabulary: 花見 and 桜 in spring, 花火 and 風鈴 in summer, 紅葉 in autumn, こたつ in winter. Haiku even require a season word (季語). The groups below are seasonal words from past puzzles. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 43 so far, newest first.

Seasonal words in Japanese

The four seasons are 春 (spring), 夏 (summer), 秋 (autumn) and 冬 (winter). Each comes with words Japanese people tie to it, and haiku must include one such season word (季語).
