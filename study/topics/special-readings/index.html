Special readings (熟字訓)

Some words are read as a whole, not kanji by kanji: 果物 is くだもの, 二十歳 is はたち. Learn them as units. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 15 so far, newest first.

Common words with special readings (熟字訓)

In these words the reading belongs to the whole word, not to each kanji: no reading of 今 plus a reading of 日 gives きょう. The official kanji list has an appendix (付表) of about a hundred such words; these are the ones learners meet first.
