Japanese verbs

Japanese verbs come in three groups, and the group decides every form. The groups below are verbs from past puzzles: verbs for the kitchen, cleaning and exercise, the る-verbs that are really godan, and the polite and plain ways to say "eat", "say" and "give". Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 23 so far, newest first.

Common Japanese verbs by group

Ichidan verbs (る-verbs) drop る: 食べる → 食べます. Godan verbs (う-verbs) change their last sound: 書く → 書きます. Only する and 来る are irregular. A verb ending in いる or える is usually ichidan, but some are godan: 帰る → 帰ります, not 帰ます.

VerbReadingMeaning and group
食べるたべるto eat: ichidan (食べます)
見るみるto see, watch: ichidan (見ます)
起きるおきるto get up: ichidan (起きます)
寝るねるto sleep, go to bed: ichidan (寝ます)
着るきるto wear (on the body): ichidan (着ます)
教えるおしえるto teach, tell: ichidan (教えます)
行くいくto go: godan (行きます; て-form 行って)
書くかくto write: godan (書きます)
話すはなすto speak: godan (話します)
飲むのむto drink: godan (飲みます)
買うかうto buy: godan (買います)
待つまつto wait: godan (待ちます)
帰るかえるto go home: godan, though it ends in える (帰ります)
入るはいるto enter: godan, though it ends in いる (入ります)
知るしるto know: godan (知ります; 知っています, I know)
切るきるto cut: godan (切ります), unlike 着る, to wear
するするto do: irregular (します); makes verbs from nouns: 勉強する
来るくるto come: irregular (来ます きます, 来ない こない)
