Greetings and set phrases in Japanese

Japanese has a set phrase for most everyday moments: leaving home, coming back, starting and finishing a meal. The groups below are greeting, thanks and apology phrases from past puzzles. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 11 so far, newest first.

En español: Saludos en japonés

Everyday Japanese greetings and when to use them

Most greetings come in pairs: いってきます is answered with いってらっしゃい, ただいま with おかえりなさい. ございます and なさい make a phrase polite; drop them with friends.

PhraseRomajiWhen you say it
おはようございますohayō gozaimasugood morning (おはよう with friends)
こんにちはkonnichiwahello; good afternoon
こんばんはkonbanwagood evening
おやすみなさいoyasumi nasaigood night (before bed)
さようならsayōnaragoodbye (when you won't see someone for a while)
またねmata nesee you (casual)
