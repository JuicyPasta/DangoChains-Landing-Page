Saying "I love you" in Japanese, and other love words

Japanese rarely says 愛してる out loud. 好き and 大好き do most of the work, and a relationship usually starts with a 告白: 好きです、付き合ってください (I like you; will you go out with me?). 恋 is falling in love; 愛 is the deeper, lasting kind.

WordReadingMeaning

愛してるあいしてる"I love you": strong, and rare in everyday speech
