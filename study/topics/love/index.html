Saying "I love you" in Japanese, and other love words

Japanese rarely says 愛してる out loud. 好き and 大好き do most of the work, and a relationship usually starts with a 告白: 好きです、付き合ってください (I like you; will you go out with me?). 恋 is falling in love; 愛 is the deeper, lasting kind.

WordReadingMeaning
好きすきto like; to love. 好きです is the classic confession
大好きだいすきto love, like a lot
愛してるあいしてる"I love you": strong, and rare in everyday speech
愛あいlove (deep, lasting)
恋こいromantic love, being in love
恋人こいびとsweetheart, partner
彼氏かれしboyfriend
彼女かのじょgirlfriend; she
片思いかたおもいa one-sided crush
初恋はつこいfirst love
一目惚れひとめぼれlove at first sight
告白こくはくconfessing your feelings (asking someone out)
付き合うつきあうto go out with, to date
デートデートa date
結婚けっこんmarriage; 結婚する, to get married
