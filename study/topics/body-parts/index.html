Japanese body parts, head to toe

To say something hurts, put the body part before が痛い: 頭が痛い (I have a headache), 歯が痛い (I have a toothache). 足 covers both the leg and the foot; 脚 is used in writing for the leg alone, and 指 is a finger or a toe (足の指).
