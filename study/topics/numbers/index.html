Numbers in Japanese

Japanese numbers are regular once you know the units (十, 百, 千, 万) and the sound changes (三百 is さんびゃく). The groups below are words that start with or hide a number reading. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 29 so far, newest first.

Japanese numbers and their readings

Numbers above ten are built from the units: 二十 (にじゅう, 20), 三百五十 (さんびゃくごじゅう, 350). Large numbers group by ten thousand (万), not by thousand, so a million is 百万. 4, 7 and 9 have two readings; よん, なな and きゅう are the usual ones when counting.
