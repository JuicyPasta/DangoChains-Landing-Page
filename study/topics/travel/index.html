Travel and transport in Japanese

Japan travels by train: 新幹線, 急行 and 各駅停車, tickets (切符) and reserved seats (指定席). The groups below add cars, buses, airports and what to pack for a trip. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 42 so far, newest first.

Getting around in Japanese

You get on with に (電車に乗る) and get off with を (電車を降りる). Trains run from the 各駅停車 that stops at every station to the 新幹線.
