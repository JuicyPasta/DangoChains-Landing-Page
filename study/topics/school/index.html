School subjects and classroom words

Subjects are named differently by school level: elementary schools teach 算数 and 図工, which become 数学 and 美術 from junior high. 化学 (chemistry) and 科学 (science in general) are both read かがく, so people sometimes say ばけがく for chemistry.
