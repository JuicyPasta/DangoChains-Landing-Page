Animals in Japanese

Pets, zoo and farm animals, birds, insects and sea creatures. Animal names are written in kanji, hiragana or katakana, and all three are common. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 92 so far, newest first.

Common animals in Japanese

Most animal names have a kanji, but everyday writing often uses hiragana (うさぎ, ねずみ) or katakana, and biology writes every species in katakana (イヌ, ネコ). Count small animals with 匹 and large ones with 頭.
