Keigo: humble and honorific verbs

Polite Japanese swaps everyday verbs for humble forms (about yourself) or honorific forms (about others). Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 15 so far, newest first.

Common keigo verbs

Honorific verbs (尊敬語) raise the person doing the action, usually a customer, a boss or a guest. Humble verbs (謙譲語) lower yourself or your side. Verbs without a special form use the patterns お〜になる (honorific) and お〜する (humble): お待ちになる, お待ちする.
