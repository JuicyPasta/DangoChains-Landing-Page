Japanese adjectives

Japanese has two kinds of adjectives, and they change form differently. The groups below are adjectives from past puzzles: tastes, textures, the な-adjectives that end in い and the ones that only look like な-adjectives. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 9 so far, newest first.

い-adjectives and な-adjectives

い-adjectives change their ending: 高い → 高くない (not expensive) → 高かった (was expensive). な-adjectives take な before a noun (静かな部屋, a quiet room) and change with です and じゃない. A few な-adjectives end in い (きれい, 嫌い), and いい is irregular.
