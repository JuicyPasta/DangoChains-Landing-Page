Countries in Japanese

Most country names are written in katakana, but newspapers shorten them to one kanji (米 for America, 英 for Britain). The groups below are country and travel-abroad words from past puzzles. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 13 so far, newest first.

Country names in Japanese

Add 人 (じん) for a person and 語 (ご) for a language: 日本人, 日本語, フランス語. English is 英語. Headlines shorten country names to one kanji, so 日米 means Japan and the US, and 訪米 a visit to the US.
