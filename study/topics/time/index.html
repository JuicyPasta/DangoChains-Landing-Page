Time in Japanese

Telling the time, parts of the day and units of time, with the reading changes that trip learners up (一分 is いっぷん, not いちふん). Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 20 so far, newest first.

Telling time in Japanese

分 is read ふん after 2, 5, 7 and 9 (にふん, ごふん) and ぷん after 1, 3, 4, 6, 8 and 10 (いっぷん, さんぷん, よんぷん, ろっぷん, はっぷん, じゅっぷん). 時 is the hour on the clock; 時間 is a length of time.
