Japanese abbreviations (略語)

Japanese clips long words and phrases: 就職活動 becomes 就活, スマートフォン becomes スマホ. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 41 so far, newest first.

Common Japanese abbreviations

Katakana words usually keep the start of each part (スマートフォン → スマホ, パーソナルコンピューター → パソコン); kanji compounds often keep one kanji from each word (就職活動 → 就活).
