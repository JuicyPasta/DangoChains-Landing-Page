Family words in Japanese

Japanese has two sets of family words: plain ones for your own family (父, 母) and polite ones for someone else's (お父さん, お母さん). The groups below cover both. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 22 so far, newest first.

En español: La familia en japonés

Japanese family words: your family and someone else's

Talking about your own family to others, use the plain words (母は元気です). For someone else's family, use the polite ones (お母さんはお元気ですか). At home, people call older relatives by the polite form too (お父さん, お姉ちゃん), and younger siblings by name. 伯父 and 伯母 are a parent's older brother and sister; 叔父 and 叔母 the younger ones; all are read おじ and おば.
