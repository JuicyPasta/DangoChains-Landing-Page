Basic food and drink words

Before a meal, say いただきます; after it, ごちそうさまでした. ご飯 means both cooked rice and a meal, so 晩ご飯 is simply dinner.

Fruits in Japanese

Most fruit names are written in kana, even when kanji exist: りんご is far more common than 林檎. Fruit as a whole is 果物 (くだもの).

みかんみかんmandarin orange

Vegetables in Japanese

Vegetables are 野菜 (やさい). Imported vegetables usually keep katakana names (キャベツ, トマト); older ones have kanji or hiragana (大根, ねぎ).
