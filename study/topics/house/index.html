Around the house in Japanese

Rooms, furniture, kitchen tools and chores: the everyday words of a Japanese home, including the 和室 (Japanese-style room) with its 畳, 障子 and 押し入れ. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 56 so far, newest first.

Rooms and things in a Japanese home

Japanese homes mix Western rooms (リビング, キッチン) with the traditional 和室: tatami floors, paper screens and a closet for the futons that are folded away each morning.
