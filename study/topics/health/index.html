Health words in Japanese

Medicine is "drunk" in Japanese, even pills: 薬を飲む. You catch a cold with ひく (風邪をひく), and say where it hurts with が痛い (頭が痛い).

WordReadingMeaning

医者いしゃdoctor
看護師かんごしnurse
