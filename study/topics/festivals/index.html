Festivals in Japanese

Summer 祭り with food stalls (屋台), Bon dancing and fireworks, and the festival days of the year, from ひな祭り in March to 七夕 in July. Every group comes from a past DangoChains puzzle; 8 so far, newest first.

Festival words in Japanese

A summer 祭り means food stalls, a 盆踊り circle, drums and often a 花火大会. The festival days below are fixed points of the Japanese year.
